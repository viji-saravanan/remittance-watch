## Honesty guardrails for copy

- Latest citable RPW quarter is **Q3 2025** — phrase as "latest available reading", never "today".
- No authoritative post-2024 flow figures exist (no Brief after #40 confirmed; circulating
  "$720B 2026 projection" claims are fabricated in aggregators — do not use).
- Bank-channel cost is **14.99%** (Q3 2025) — older "~11–12%" citations are stale.
